Privacy

A deliberately simple policy for a deliberately simple first version of the site.

← Return to the guide

Current site behaviour

The eligibility screen runs in your browser. Its answers are not sent to us or stored by the site. We do not use advertising cookies or user accounts.

Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, traffic sources and broad interaction patterns such as opening the publication-list form, using the deadline tool or completing the eligibility screen. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics”. We do not send email addresses, eligibility answers, tax information or other sensitive information to Google Analytics.

You may refuse analytics without affecting access to the site. Your choice is stored in your browser so that the notice is not shown on every page.

We also use Yandex Metrica to measure page visits, traffic sources and link interactions. Yandex Metrica loads when a page is opened and may use browser identifiers, cookies, local storage, IP-derived location, device and browser information. Session Replay, form analytics and Webvisor are disabled. Yandex processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Publication list form

If you join the publication list, the form sends your email address through FormSubmit and then forwards the submission to our mailbox at hello@turkeynondom.com. We use the address for the guide release notice, material Article 20/D updates and closely related service messages. Do not send passports, tax returns, bank statements or other sensitive documents through this form.

Email contact

If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours process the message. We use the information to answer the request, organise a requested service and maintain necessary business records.

Hosting data

The hosting provider may process basic server logs, including IP address, browser information and request time, for security and service operation. Its own terms and privacy information apply.

Future changes

If we add advertising, a newsletter platform or materially different analytics in the future, we will update this policy and any required consent controls before activating them.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to hello@turkeynondom.com.
